## Incident response

1. **Detection** — We look for signs that information has been exposed.
2. **Assessment** — The situation is judged for severity.
3. **Containment** — Exposure is limited as far as we can.
4. **Notification** — Affected parties are informed within 24 hours when notice is required.
5. **Resolution** — The issue is remediated and recorded.
6. **Prevention** — The way of working is updated so the same failure is harder to repeat.

## Zoharav Privacy Policy

**1. Introduction.** At Zoharav, we value the trust of our clients, partners, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information obtained through our website and direct interactions.

**2. Information we collect.** We may collect contact details (name, email, phone number, company); information voluntarily submitted via forms or email; and technical data (IP address, browser, device type) collected through cookies and analytics.

**3. Use of information.** We use collected information to respond to inquiries and contact requests; facilitate networking and potential partnerships; improve the experience on our website; and share informational or corporate updates, with prior consent.

**4. Information sharing.** We do not sell or trade personal data. Information may only be shared when necessary for the execution of a contract or partnership, when required by law or a competent authority, or when explicit consent has been granted.

**5. Data security.** We implement technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. No online transmission is entirely secure, and users acknowledge this inherent risk.

**6. Data retention.** Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by applicable law.

**7. User rights.** Depending on jurisdiction, users may exercise rights of access, rectification, deletion, opposition, or restriction of processing. Requests can be made through Zoharav’s official contact channels.

**8. Cookies and analytics.** Our site may use cookies for statistical and functional purposes. Users can manage or disable cookies through their browser settings, though this may limit site functionality.

**9. Policy updates.** Zoharav may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date of the latest version will be indicated.

**10. Contact.** For privacy inquiries: info@zoharav.com. Offices referenced in this policy: San Juan (PR), Miami (USA).
