# Refund Policy

## 1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to services and commercial relationships formalized in writing between Zoharav and its clients or partners.

## 2. Nature of services

Our services are consulting, strategic advisory, and support in forming business relationships. Because the work is intangible and tailored, fees for services already underway are not refundable.

## 3. Online reservation of the strategic session

<!-- TODO: revisar con legal -->

The website may collect payment for a strategic consulting session of USD $500. That amount reserves a 60-minute session, virtual or in person, and is credited toward the full engagement if the client proceeds. It is not a purchase of oil, gas, minerals, or any physical good.

Until legal counsel confirms this clause, the following is the working rule:

- If the session has not been held and the client cancels in writing at least 5 business days before the scheduled time, the reservation is refunded.
- If Zoharav cancels and does not offer a reasonable new time, the reservation is refunded.
- If the session has been held, the fee is earned. If the client later proceeds with an engagement, the amount is credited as described in the proposal, not refunded in cash.
- Payment disputes follow the rules of the payment provider used at checkout.

## 4. Other refund conditions

A refund of a contracted engagement may be considered only when a contract has been signed and no services have commenced, or when Zoharav is unable, for reasons attributable to us, to perform. Any refund follows the contract.

## 5. Exclusions

No refund is due when services have commenced or deliverables have been provided; when delay comes from the client’s lack of cooperation or information; or when the contract states that the fees are non-refundable.

## 6. How to ask

Requests must be sent in writing to info@zoharav.com and must include the client or company name, the contract or reservation reference, and the reason. Zoharav will respond within ten (10) business days.

## 7. Updates

Zoharav may update this policy. The effective date of the latest version will be shown on this page.
