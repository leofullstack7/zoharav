# Damage Policy

<!-- TODO: revisar con legal. The previous text spoke of “digital products”. The firm no longer sells technology services. This draft keeps the non-physical nature of consulting and removes product-shop language. -->

Zoharav delivers consulting and written advice. We do not ship physical goods. In this policy, “damage” means a failure in the delivery of the advisory work itself.

## What qualifies

- A service interruption, attributable to Zoharav, longer than 72 hours that prevents an agreed deliverable from being received
- Loss or corruption of client materials caused by an internal error of the firm
- Delivery of a work product that is materially incomplete against the signed scope

## What does not qualify

- A disagreement about the scope
- A decision the client does not implement
- Errors by a third party the client hires to carry out the advice
- Market outcomes, commodity prices, or regulatory results

Report a concern within 3 business days of delivery to support@zoharav.com.
